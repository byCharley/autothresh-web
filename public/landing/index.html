Separation Modes

Six ways to separate.
Every print covered.

From traditional spot-color halftones to ICC-grade process CMYK and direct-to-garment — pick the mode that matches your press and your artwork.

Thresh
Spot Color

Tonal threshold layers, one ink per band. Fully adjustable ranges, paint tools, and knockout. The standard for traditional screen printing.

Dither
Simulated Process

Simulate full-color gradients using halftone patterns. Fewer inks, more perceived color through optical mixing. Great for photorealistic and DTG prints.

Color
OKLAB Clustering

Perceptual color clustering in OKLAB space. Groups pixels by actual hue — reds stay red, blues stay blue. Ideal for color-accurate spot-color prints.

CMYK
ICC Process

Full ICC-based RGB→CMYK via LittleCMS. Industry profiles (SWOP, FOGRA39), GCR/UCR, dot gain, TAC ink limiting, and AM halftone screening.

DTG / DTF
Direct to Garment

Knocks out heavy backgrounds for direct-to-garment and direct-to-film printing. Built-in halftone screen, white channel, and realistic fabric preview.

Texture
Surface Overlays

Layer grunge, film grain, chalkboard, and halftone surfaces on any separation with advanced blend modes. For distressed, retro, and hand-printed aesthetics.

Features

Live Halftone Preview

See exactly how your dots will print before you export. Adjust LPI, screen angle, dot gain, and ink density in real time — no guessing.

Background Removal

Auto-remove backgrounds in one click. Fine-tune with the eyedropper (click any color to globally erase it) or paint directly on the mask.

Paint & Erase Tools

Manually paint or erase any layer with a brush. Add ink to highlights, punch holes in shadows, or refine edges — complete creative control.

Reg Marks + Bleed

Registration marks and document bleed built in. Every export is print-ready with proper marks, sizing, and DPI — hand it straight to your printer.

Presets System

Save your full layer setup — colors, thresholds, patterns — as a preset. Stored to your account, available across every device, loaded in one click.

Garment Mockup

Preview your art on an actual t-shirt before you export. Drag to reposition, adjust scale, and download a mockup PNG to share with clients faster.

DTG Halftone Engine

Export Formats

Your files, your way.

Whether you're on Photoshop, CorelDRAW, or sending straight to a RIP — we've got an export for that.

PNG
ZIP of PNGs

One transparent PNG per color layer + composite proof. Universal, lossless.

PSD
Photoshop

Layered PSD — one layer per ink, named with hex codes. Open in Photoshop immediately.

CDR
CorelDRAW

Numbered EPS plates with spot-color DSC headers + a step-by-step import guide.

PDF
Multi-Page PDF

One page per separation with reg marks. Compatible with any RIP or imposition software.

TIFF
ZIP of TIFFs

High-depth TIFF files per separation. Preferred by many film-output and DTS workflows.

EPS
EPS ZIP

Grayscale EPS per separation with spot-color DSC headers. Works with AccuRIP, Kothari, Separation Studio.
